# 90-degree PVC structural elbow

An L-shaped joint for two PVC pipes, with a solid triangular gusset filling
the inside corner to strengthen the turn. Each pipe enters its own blind socket
and can be retained by a screw through the top wall. These are **blind**
sockets: the centre is solid; there is no fluid passage between the pipes.

The two collars meet at 90° in the XY plane. The gusset is solid from bed to
the top of the collars; both socket cavities are cut back out of it. One
top-facing hole per socket goes through only the upper 4 mm wall into the pipe
pocket. The screw should engage the PVC, not the opposite printed wall. The
underside is clipped flat by 0.8 mm, leaving at least 3.2 mm of material
beneath either socket.

## Fit trial

The 14.6 mm OD pipe would not enter either the first 15.1 mm nominal socket or
the subsequent 15.6 mm coupon. A printed **16.4 mm nominal** ASA coupon fitted
the pipe, so both the full elbow and its current coupon now use that bore. The
successful trial coupon was cut from the earlier, smaller 23.6 mm OD parent,
leaving 3.6 mm radial wall. The final model grows the outside to 24.4 mm so
its wall is the requested 4 mm; it has not yet been printed at this outside
diameter. The current coupon is trimmed directly from the final elbow and
shares its 30 mm insertion, screw hole, underside and print orientation.

ASA shrinkage or warping and horizontal-bore support contact can both reduce
the usable printed opening. Clean support out of the sockets before fitting;
the screw provides retention. If it binds only farther in, check for remaining
support or sagging at the socket ceiling.

Build the current coupon with `mise build pvc-elbow coupon` if you want to
recheck the fit against the final wall thickness before printing the full elbow.

## Print settings

ASA is the user's print material. Lay the two flat underside strips on the bed
(model orientation); print both horizontal sockets with supports removable via
the two open mouths (`SUPPORT = True`). The pipe fit was validated on the
16.4 mm trial coupon; the full elbow and screw fit have not been printed and
validated yet.

Build with `mise build pvc-elbow`; verify with `mise test pvc-elbow` and
`mise check pvc-elbow`.
