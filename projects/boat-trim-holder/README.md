# Boat trim holder

Replacement for a friend's boat trim holder: a constant-width arched strip
with short shoulders and two downturned ends. The two holes pass through the
4.5 mm shoulder/arch strip. **There are no inward feet at the bottom.**

The mating trim and mounting surface are unavailable. The references are the
hand sketch and three photographs in `inputs/boat-trim-piece/`, plus the user's
corrections. Those local reference images are not committed or required to
build. `blockout-spec.md` records the corrected topology and visual checks.

## Decisions and remaining unknowns

The main arch is made from two mirrored **quintic Bézier curves**, with zero
slope and curvature where they leave the level shoulders and matching slope
and curvature at the crown. This replaces the visibly bumped joins between
the previous shoulder curves and circular crown. The user relaxed the radius
constraint after reviewing that version, so 46 mm is retained in the source
only as a reference, not enforced as a circle.

The inner contour is a **4.5 mm normal offset** of the upper profile, keeping
the strip thickness through the slopes. Cubic end-corner blends transition
to the 3.2 mm straight ends. All curves are extruded uniformly across 37 mm.
The assumed corner reach, shoulder length and Bézier handles are recorded in
`measurements.toml` as provisional design estimates, not photo measurements.

The holes run along Y through the upper shoulders at X = 14.65 and 87.35 mm,
Z = 18.5 mm. Their centres are 72.7 mm apart. No countersinks or dimensional
clearances are inferred. The first physical comparison must establish fit.

The earlier “20 mm inward lip” answer followed an incorrect topology proposed
by the agent. The user's later explicit correction removes that feature;
20 mm is retained only for the independently confirmed end-region height.

## Geometry checks

The model checks its solid validity, envelope and bed fit; measures finished
crown and shoulder thickness/underside height; checks both 3.2 mm end tips to
prevent bottom feet returning; and verifies that both holes pierce the upper
strip at the specified diameter and edge offsets. The project suite also
checks the exported mesh for watertightness and volume agreement.

To compare directly with the side-profile photograph, look along **Z** in the
viewer, or open `exports/boat-trim-holder/renders/holder-top.png`. The name
“top” refers to the print orientation, not the orientation on the boat.

## Printing

ASA for outdoor/boat exposure. Print with a cross-section face on the bed:
the entire open arch then grows vertically across its full 37 mm width,
with the screw holes running horizontally through 4.5 mm of material.
Start with a 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.2 mm layers, in an enclosure; none of these
settings or the fit has been validated on a physical print. Use
`mise check boat-trim-holder` before printing and `mise slice boat-trim-holder` for a
slicer-based estimate.
